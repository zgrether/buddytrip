# The app when the redesign is done: a scope brief for brand and marketing

*Written 2026-11-15 for the branding and marketing conversation. It describes the product as it
will be once the redesign in `claude/REDESIGN_PLAN.md` ships, in plain words, with no build
detail. Where something exists today, it says so. Nothing here decides the name or the look;
see "What the brand still has to decide" at the end.*

---

## In one line

**The place your crews keep score, all year.** A fast score sheet for any game, kept per group
of people, so every game adds to a running record; plus competitions and trips for the bigger
occasions.

## Who it's for

Groups of people who already play together and argue about who's winning:

- **A family** (Mom, Dad & me): Scrabble, Hearts, cribbage at the kitchen table.
- **Golf buddies** (BBMI, ~28 people): a yearly trip with a team cup, plus weekend rounds.
- **A regular night** (Poker night, bar trivia, darts).
- **An office or friend group** running a pick'em through a season.

Most of them are not tech-savvy. The person who sets things up is usually one organizer; everyone
else just wants to tap a score and see who's ahead.

**Not for, at launch:** strangers (a 200-person open pool is a later idea; see below), betting or moving
money (the app tracks who owes whom; it never handles payments), or pro/league sports admin.

## The words the app uses (the brand should use them too)

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Crew** | A group of people who play together. Everything lives in a crew. A person can be in many crews. |
| **Game** | One game of anything, scored on a sheet. "Mom won game 5." |
| **Series** | Best of 3/5/7 between the same people. "You lead 3–2." |
| **Cup** / **Tournament** | A competition: a Cup is team against team (head to head); a Tournament is points across many games. A **Bracket** is a knockout format inside one. A season-long **League** comes later. |
| **Trip** | Going somewhere together: where, when, where to stay, the plan, the money. A trip can hold a Cup. |
| **Events** | One tab listing every competition and trip you're in, past and coming. |
| **Member · Helper · Admin** | The three roles in a crew. A crew can have several admins. |
| **Live** | Happening right now. Always red **with its dot and the word Live**; a team may also be red. |

Copy rules already set by Zach (keep them in marketing too): US spelling. Few words; show state by
styling, not sentences. "Won", never "takes". "Cancel", never "abandon". No real event names
(never "Ryder Cup"). Points are "awarded", never "paid" (money words are for money only). Every
score sheet says TOTAL.

## What people do, most used first

1. **Keep score of any game.** Pick the game, tap who's playing, score. Works with no account;
   sign up to save it and play with others. Cards, board games, dice, yard games, golf. Each game
   scores its own way (count up, who went out, bid and make, Hearts' 26, Yahtzee's boxes).
2. **Crews.** Every game you play with the same people adds to their record: wins, head to head,
   the games list, all year and year after year. *This is the reason to sign up.*
3. **Series and rematches.** Make any game a best-of, run it back in one tap, end a series early.
   "Play again" always continues an open series.
4. **Competitions.** Team cups and points tournaments with a live board: standings, the clinch
   watch, the winner's podium. *Exists today and is the most distinctive thing the app does.*
5. **Golf.** Scorecards with courses and handicaps; stroke, match play, skins, scramble,
   rack-n-stack, side bets and settling up. *Exists today and is deep.*
6. **Trips.** Ideas and votes for where, a date poll, lodging, the agenda, News from the
   organizer, receipts split among everyone. *Exists today; the app's origin.*
7. **Trip mode.** Three days before a trip, everyone's app opens straight to the trip: today's
   plan, live games, the cup. After, the trip becomes a one-page recap.

## The moments worth showing (the brand's best material)

- **The first game:** no sign-up, a score sheet in three taps, and at the end "Mom won".
- **LIVE NOW on Home:** a red panel with the game you're in, mid-score.
- **The series card:** dots for each game, "You lead 3–2", one button: Start game 6.
- **The finished game:** the result in one line, the sheet, one button to play again.
- **The crew's record:** head to head, wins this year, every game ever played together.
- **The cup board on the trip:** two teams racing to a number, the clinch, the podium.
- **Show on TV:** the board on the big screen at the house.
- **The recap:** who won the cup, every game, who came, all settled.

## Where it runs

- **Phone first** (a web app you add to the home screen). Five tabs: Home · Crews · + · Events · You.
- **Desktop:** a left rail with your crews, Home in two columns, a full-width score sheet.
- **TV:** a read-only board for the room.

## Where it's headed (later, but the brand has to fit it)

The redesign is built so these can be added without starting over (`claude/FUTURE_PATTERNS.md`
has the detail). None ship at launch; all of them should still sound right under the name.

- **Judged and voted nights.** The idea started with the family's Thanksgiving blind wine
  tasting: eight hidden bottles, 20–25 tasters, a card per person, and a reveal at the end. The
  same shape covers a bourbon or beer flight, a chili or BBQ cook-off, a pie contest, trivia
  night, and an awards ballot ("best shot of the trip", "MVP of the weekend"). The host picks a
  template ("Help me host a wine night"), paces it ("Now pouring #3"), and everyone fills in
  their own card on their phone, or on a printed card the host types in.
- **Three kinds of winner from one night:** the people (who won), the things (the group's
  favorite wine, the best chili), and who was closest to the truth (best palate, most trivia
  right; pick'em already works this way).
- **The reveal as the moment the night builds to,** using the same celebration as a cup's
  winner's circle.
- **Join in seconds:** a QR code or a 4-letter code at the table, a name, and you're in. No
  account for the night; claim it later.
- **The TV board:** the room watches the standings, the score sheet or the tasting reveal on
  the big screen, paired with the same code.
- **Leagues:** a season-long pick'em in a crew, ending in something like Super Bowl squares.
- **Big open groups** (a 200-person pool where not everyone knows each other): a second kind of
  crew, much later.
- **Every year again:** "Thanksgiving tasting 2026, 2027…" becomes part of the crew's history,
  the way BBMI's trips are.

Voting already exists in a small way today: a trip's ideas and the date poll are voted on, and
pick'em is picks scored against the results.

**What this means for the name:** the app isn't only "keep score" between people. It also judges
things, votes, ranks favorites and reveals answers. A name that only means a fight between
people ("battle", "versus") or only a running count will fit the score sheet and fit a tasting
badly. Test every candidate on "Thanksgiving wine tasting on ___" as well as "Scrabble on ___".

## What already exists vs. what's new

Built and in use today (BBMI ran its 2026 cup on it): trips end to end, team cups and points
tournaments, brackets, pick'em, golf in depth, receipts and splits, News, invites.

New in the redesign: crews as the home for everything, the everyday score sheet for any game,
series, the new Home, Events, the finished-game screen, desktop, trip mode, archived crews.

## Brand constraints that come from the product

- **It's a scoreboard.** Scores are the biggest thing on any screen they're on.
- **Read in sunlight at arm's length** (a phone on a golf course is the hardest moment).
- **Color belongs to teams and crews.** Each crew picks a color and an emoji or photo; teams have
  colors. Brand color has to sit alongside eight team colors without clashing.
- **Each game wears its own look** (a token and color per game type).
- **Live is red with its dot and the word.** A team can be red too (ruling in `claude/PALETTE.md`); gold only ever means won.
- **It has to work for a family as well as a golf group.** Golf is the deepest feature but not
  the identity; no golf-only or betting vocabulary in the name or tagline.

## The brand (decided 2026-11-16)

**Put Me Down For (PMDF)**, "Same crew. Every game. Anywhere." Name, logo, palette, type and copy
rules are in `docs/design/BRAND.md` (in the project as `claude/BRAND.md`). Still open: trademark,
the domain, final artwork.
